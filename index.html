    How To Play

    Controls

    Keyboard

    • ↑ Move Up
    • ↓ Move Down
    • ← Move Left
    • → Move Right
    • WASD works too
    • Space or P to pause

    Touch/Click

    • Tap/click the grid to direct the snake
    • Swipe anywhere on the grid
    • Use on-screen controls (toggle with )

    Game Rules

    • Guide the snake to eat the blue squares
    • Each food eaten adds 10 points
    • A yellow square appears after every 4th food. It's worth 30 but disappears quickly
    • Snake grows longer with each food eaten, and speeds up every 5 foods
    • Game ends if snake hits a wall (in Walls mode) or itself

    Game Mode

    Install

    Add the game to your home screen or desktop. It works offline.

    On iPhone or iPad: tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
