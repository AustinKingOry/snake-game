    How to play

    Controls

    Keyboard

    • Arrow keys or WASD to steer
    • Space or P to pause
    • R to restart
    • M to mute

    Touch

    • Swipe on the board
    • Or use the on-screen controls (cross button)

    Install

    Add Nyoka to your home screen or desktop. It works offline.

    On iPhone or iPad: tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.

    Rules

    • Eat mangoes: 10 points each, and you grow.
    • A golden mango appears after every 4th fruit. It's worth 30 but fades fast.
    • Every 5 fruit raises the level and the snake speeds up.
    • Walls mode: the edge ends your run. Wrap mode: the edges loop around.
    • Hitting yourself always ends the run.
